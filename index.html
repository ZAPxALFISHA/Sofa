Ashraf Sofas began as a small upholstery workshop with a simple belief: a sofa should be the most comfortable and most beautiful place in a home.

Today our artisans build new sofas from the frame up, restore treasured pieces that families refuse to part with, and design bespoke furniture to the millimetre. We use hardwood frames, resilient foam and fabrics chosen for how they age, and every sofa is inspected by the master upholsterer before it leaves the studio.
